# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

**Step 1 — Accept the Microsoft invitation**
- Open the “Microsoft Invitations…” message and click **Accept invitation**.
- The invitation screen notes: if you were **not expecting** this invitation, **proceed with caution**.

  
![Step 1 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)

**Step 2 — Sign in with your password**
- On the Microsoft sign-in page, enter your password and click **Sign in**.

  
![Step 2 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)

**Step 3 — Approve the permissions request**
- On the “Permissions requested by …” screen, click **Accept**.

  
![Step 3 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)

**Step 4 — Start Microsoft Authenticator setup (computer)**
- When you see **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator**, select **Next**.
- Use **Show QR code** to display the QR code you will scan with your phone.

  
![Step 4 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

**Step 5 — Add your work/school account in the Authenticator app (phone)**
- In Microsoft Authenticator, go to **Add account**.
- Select **Work or school account**.

  
![Step 5 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_2.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=PQUlk9qPPTBG/7ZDSUxrcEjk2IhZHWi2eLvcu0G1640%3D)

**Step 6 — Scan the QR code**
- In the Authenticator app, choose **Scan QR code**.
- Scan the QR code shown on your computer.

  
![Step 6 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

**Step 7 — Approve the notification (number matching)**
- When prompted to approve the notification, enter the number shown on-screen (example shown: **62**) to approve.

  
![Step 7 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=sqNzJVPL1NodpWdJJyz7XNhi%2BcE9B2JsS%2B3KzvdJGrY%3D)

**Step 8 — (If prompted) Verify your identity using Email**
- If you see “We need to verify your identity,” select **Email** and click **Get code**.
- Retrieve the emailed code and enter it when prompted.

  
![Step 8 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=sqNzJVPL1NodpWdJJyz7XNhi%2BcE9B2JsS%2B3KzvdJGrY%3D)

**Step 9 — (Optional) Add an additional verification method**
- On **Add a method**, open **Choose a method** and select one of the available options shown in the document.

  
![Step 9 visual](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=sqNzJVPL1NodpWdJJyz7XNhi%2BcE9B2JsS%2B3KzvdJGrY%3D)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What “Add a method” options are shown?**
  - Authenticator app
  - Email
  - Security key
  - Office phone
- **What if I can’t use the selected verification option?**
  - The screen shows an option **“I don’t have any of these”**. Follow the on-screen prompts. (Additional instructions are not shown in the source.)
