# Barracuda Backup Policy v1.0 (End-User Guide)

## Introduction / Objective
Barracuda Backup is 1440 Foods’ solution for backing up emails and user files. It allows end users to recover lost or deleted information. This guide explains how to restore or export individual files or emails using Barracuda Backup.

## Instructions: Restore Email/File in Barracuda Backup

### Step 1 — Navigate to the Desired Service
In Barracuda Backup, click on the **Protect** tab at the top. From the drop-down menu, select the Microsoft service you want to restore data from. (In the source example, an email deleted on June 3, 2024 is being restored, but the process applies to other services.)

### Step 2 — Select the Email/File
After you find the email/file you want, click the **checkmark** on the left of the email/file name, then click **Restore** in the top right of the window.

### Step 3 — Choose the Restore Location
A popup will appear prompting you to choose where to restore the email/file:

- **Original Location:** Restores the item to its original location.  
- **Other Location:** Allows you to select a new destination for the restored item.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/processed_images/visual_f5f4_page_5.png?st=2026-01-20T19%3A25%3A18Z&se=2027-01-20T19%3A30%3A18Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=HFIG3/0TfIigr5br3xlzA3brqhuwVlz/HA0POJ1mCdo%3D

### Step 4 — Monitor the Restore Process
To track progress, go to the **Dashboard**. About halfway down the page, find the box labeled **Restore** where status is displayed. Once complete, a **green checkmark** appears confirming the restore. You can then navigate to the chosen location to access the restored email or file.

## Instructions: Export Email/File in Barracuda Backup

### Step 1 — Follow Initial Steps
Begin by following the same steps as outlined for restoration (navigate to the desired service and select the email/file).

### Step 2 — Select Export
Instead of selecting **Restore**, choose **Export** after selecting the desired email or file.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/processed_images/visual_eebc_page_7.png?st=2026-01-20T19%3A25%3A19Z&se=2027-01-20T19%3A30%3A19Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fSyuLeKw01pmDb%2BRigMyYBK/3xRlcbOYsKBApwQXYgE%3D

### Step 3 — Monitor the Export Process
Return to the **Dashboard** and scroll down to the **Export** section to track progress. Once the export is complete, the file will appear in your **downloads folder**. Double click to upload into outlook.
