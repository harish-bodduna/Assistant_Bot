# 1440 Foods — Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

**Step 1 — Accept the Microsoft invitation**  
In the invitation email, select **Accept invitation**.

  
![Accept invitation](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D)

**Step 2 — Sign in with your password**  
On the Microsoft sign-in screen, enter your password and select **Sign in**.

  
![Enter password and sign in](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)

**Step 3 — Accept the permissions request**  
On the **Permissions requested** screen, select **Accept**.

  
![Permissions requested - Accept](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D)

**Step 4 — Start Microsoft Authenticator setup (on your computer)**  
On the **Keep your account secure** screen, select **Show QR code**, then select **Next**.

  
![Keep your account secure - Show QR code / Next](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

**Step 5 — Add your work/school account in the Authenticator app (on your phone)**  
In Microsoft Authenticator, choose **Work or school account**.

  
![Add account - Work or school account](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D)

**Step 6 — Choose to scan a QR code**  
Select **Scan QR code**.

  
![Scan QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D)

**Step 7 — Scan the QR code shown on your computer**  
Use your phone camera (inside Authenticator) to scan the QR code.

  
![QR code to scan](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)

**Step 8 — Approve the test notification (number match)**  
When prompted, approve the notification by entering the **number shown on your computer screen** in the Authenticator prompt.

  
![Number match prompt](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)


## Optional: Email Code Verification Screens (if prompted)

**Optional Step 9 — Choose “Email code” on the sign-in screen**  
If you see an **Email code** option on the password screen, select it.

  
![Email code option](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

**Optional Step 10 — Request a security code by email**  
On the **We need to verify your identity** screen, select **Get code**.

  
![Get code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)


## Optional: Add/Change an MFA Method (Security Info)

**Optional Step 11 — Add a method**  
If you see the **Add a method** window, open **Choose a method** and select one of the available options shown (e.g., **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, **Office phone**).

  
![Add a method - Choose a method](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_aabc.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=SWHWJ9/7lFrzRy8ubcNmgDxwMunjVZkfxJ1eM45zE1U%3D)
