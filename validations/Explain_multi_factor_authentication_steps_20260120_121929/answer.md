# Microsoft Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

1. Open the Microsoft invitation email and select **Accept invitation** (the email notes to proceed with caution if you were not expecting the invitation).  

![Image_d6eb.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D)

2. Enter your password and select **Sign in**.  

![Image_564d.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)

3. On the **Permissions requested by Worldwide Sport Nutritional Supplements (1440foods.onmicrosoft.com)** screen, select **Accept**.  

![Image_3e6e.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D)

4. On the **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator** screen, select **Next** to begin setting up your account in the app.  

![Image_dfc8.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D)

5. Display the QR code (select **Show QR code**), so you can pair your account to Microsoft Authenticator.  

![Image_0ad0.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D)

6. In the Microsoft Authenticator app, select **Work or school account** (from the **Add account** screen).  

![Image_5d9c.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)

7. When prompted, select **Scan QR code**.  

![Image_b5be.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_b5be.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=6EHQj3yGEXuAKQ026pEbXbU711dmJpMxG9ZY2HiOeRs%3D)

8. Approve the Microsoft Authenticator prompt by entering the number shown on the screen (example shown: **62**).  

![Image_ab05.png (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

9. If you are prompted with **We need to verify your identity**, choose **Email** and select **Get code**.  

![Image_9bd9.png (page 2)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

10. Enter the **Email code** on the sign-in screen to continue.  

![Image_0657.png (page 2)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

11. To add another MFA method, open **Add a method** and use **Choose a method** to select from the available options shown (Authenticator app, Email, Security key, Office phone).  

![Image_61b0.png (page 4)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)
