# Microsoft Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

**Step 1 — Accept the Microsoft invitation**  
Open the invitation and click **Accept invitation**.

![Accept invitation](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)


**Step 2 — Sign in with your Microsoft password**  
Enter your password and select **Sign in**.

![Enter password and sign in](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)


**Step 3 — Approve the organization permissions**  
Review the permissions request and click **Accept**.

![Permissions requested – Accept](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D)


**Step 4 — Choose an MFA method to add**  
In the **Add a method** window, select the method you want to add (for example, **Authenticator app**).

![Add a method](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)


**Step 5 — Start Microsoft Authenticator setup (on your computer)**  
On **Keep your account secure – Microsoft Authenticator**, follow the prompt to set up your account in the app and click **Next**. Use **Show QR code** to display the QR code for pairing.

![Set up your account in the app – Next](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)


**Step 6 — In the Authenticator app (on your phone), add your work/school account**  
In Microsoft Authenticator, choose **Work or school account**.

![Add account – Work or school account](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)


**Step 7 — Scan the QR code**  
On your phone, select **Scan QR code**, then scan the QR code shown on your computer.

![Scan QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

![QR code (computer screen)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)


**Step 8 — Approve the sign-in (number matching)**  
When prompted, approve the notification by entering the number shown on the screen (example shown: **62**).

![Approve notification by entering the number shown](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_b5be.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=6EHQj3yGEXuAKQ026pEbXbU711dmJpMxG9ZY2HiOeRs%3D)


## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What MFA methods are shown as available to add?**  
  - Authenticator app  
  - Email  
  - Security key  
  - Office phone

- **If I’m prompted to verify by email, what do I do?**  
  - Select **Email** and click **Get code**.  
  - Enter the **Email code** when prompted.
