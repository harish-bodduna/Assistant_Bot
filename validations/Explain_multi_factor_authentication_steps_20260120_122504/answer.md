# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator) — 1440 Foods / WSNS

## Instructions

1. In the Microsoft invitation email, select **Accept invitation**.

   ![Accept invitation](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D)

2. When prompted, enter your password and select **Sign in**.

   ![Enter password and sign in](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)

3. On the **Permissions requested by Worldwide Sport Nutritional Supplements** screen, select **Accept**.

   ![Permissions requested - Accept](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D)

4. When you reach **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator**, select **Next** to begin setup.

   ![Keep your account secure - Next](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_b5be.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=6EHQj3yGEXuAKQ026pEbXbU711dmJpMxG9ZY2HiOeRs%3D)

5. Select **Show QR code** to display the QR code on your computer.

   ![Show QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

6. In the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone, go to **Add account** and select **Work or school account**.

   ![Add account - Work or school account](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D)

7. Choose **Scan QR code**.

   ![Scan QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D)

8. Scan the QR code displayed on your computer to pair your account. Then, when prompted to approve the notification, enter the number shown on screen.

   ![Approve notification by entering the number shown](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)

9. If you are prompted to verify your identity by email, select **Email** and click **Get code**.

   ![We need to verify your identity - Get code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

10. Enter the code you received (shown as **Email code** on the sign-in screen), then continue sign-in.

   ![Enter Email code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)

11. To add another MFA option later, use **Add a method** and choose a method from the list shown (for example: **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, **Office phone**).

   ![Add a method dropdown](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What methods can be added for MFA?
The **Add a method** list shows these options: **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, and **Office phone**.
