# Signing into 1440 Foods with Microsoft Authenticator (Multi-Factor Authentication)

Two-factor authentication adds another authentication method to the sign-in process along with your password, making it much more difficult for a hacker or other malicious party to gain access to your account even if your password is compromised.

## Instructions (MFA Setup)

### Step 1: Accept the invitation
Open the email from 1440 Foods inviting you to join a Teams channel or SharePoint server and select **Accept Invitation**.

  
![e8ae](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/processed_images/visual_e8ae_page_1.png?st=2026-01-20T19%3A24%3A13Z&se=2027-01-20T19%3A29%3A13Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=ptjVBqVpm6ohlT/W3/0mCNJ4XkAQsZ46d5l7oNV5UB4%3D)

### Step 2: Sign in with your credentials
Provide your user credentials and password. Use the sign-in credentials from the account that received the email.

### Step 3: Grant 1440 Foods permission
Grant 1440 Foods permission to view your profile data to confirm access. Select **Accept**.

### Step 4: Prepare Microsoft Authenticator on your mobile device
Open the **Microsoft Authenticator** app on your mobile device. Select **Work or school account**, then **Scan QR code**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How can I change/add/delete multifactor authentication methods?
**A:** Go to **aka.ms/mfasetup** to change/add/delete accounts from the MFA application. This is usually used if the user wants to add additional accounts, delete an inactive/wrong account, or make changes to an account to ensure seamless access to files/workspace at any given time.  
If you have any trouble, please follow this link for a detailed step-by-step setup process: https://youtu.be/uWbkLuI4g30?si=noZzjI8Ph_yTwOgS

### Q: What is my Microsoft account password?
**A:** If your organization uses Microsoft, the password will be the same. If you are not sure what your password is, you can sign in via a code sent to your email.

### Q: Will I have to set up authentication every time I access a shared file?
**A:** No, you will only have to set up authentication once. However, you will need to re-authenticate on each device and each browser you use to access a shared file/workspace.

### Q: Are there other authentication methods besides scanning a QR code with the app?
**A:** Users can choose how they want to authenticate. Options include the Authenticator app (preferred), email, security key, and office phone. To learn more about ways to authenticate, please visit **aka.ms/mfasetup**.

### Q: Is it possible to reset all my authentication methods if I don't have access to any of them?
**A:** In the event you find yourself unable to access your chosen authentication methods, send an email to **itsupport@1440Foods.com**. The team will respond to your query and make sure it is resolved. For best results please read the other sections of this FAQ, as your issue may be explained in a later section.

### Q: What happens if I do not receive a code?
**A:** When creating your account, it's advisable to incorporate additional Multi-Factor authentication methods, such as an authenticator app and SMS verification. Should you encounter difficulties receiving a code or accessing any of your chosen options, kindly reach out to **itsupport@1440Foods.com**.

### Q: I already have another authentication app on my smart device. Why should I add the Microsoft Authenticator app?
**A:** The Microsoft Authenticator app employs push notifications for authentication, offering a faster and more convenient method compared to entering numeric codes used by other authenticator apps. Employees/IT support are well-versed in assisting users who utilize the Microsoft Authenticator app.

### Q: What should I do if I get an authentication request and I'm not trying to log in?
**A:** If you haven't tried to log into any Company systems, never authorize an authentication request from Microsoft Authenticator. Decline the request immediately and notify the IT Support team, as your password may be compromised. In the event you accidentally approve such a request, contact technical support right away. Contact: **itsupport@1440Foods.com**

### Q: How often will I be asked to re-authenticate?
**A:** If you're using the same device, network, and browser (without private mode), and your account is not suspected of being compromised, you should only have to re-authenticate every 90 days. If you clear your cache, browsing history, or sign out of your applications, you will have to re-authenticate again.
