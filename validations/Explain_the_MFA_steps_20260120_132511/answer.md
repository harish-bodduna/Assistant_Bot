# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

1. In the Microsoft invitation email, click **Accept invitation**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D

2. When prompted, enter your password and click **Sign in**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D

3. On the **Permissions requested by: Worldwide Sport Nutritional Supplements** screen, click **Accept**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D

4. On the **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator** setup screen, click **Next** to continue.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D

5. Click **Show QR code** to display the QR code you will scan with the Microsoft Authenticator app.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D

6. Confirm the QR code is visible on-screen (you will scan this in the next steps).

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D

7. In the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone, select **Add account**, then choose **Work or school account**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_aabc.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=SWHWJ9/7lFrzRy8ubcNmgDxwMunjVZkfxJ1eM45zE1U%3D

8. Choose **Scan QR code**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_e3fc.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=pXmjJVQHuv3DXBcARrDxNbVxtwV8JSAOFl/CFkGHLO4%3D

9. Use your phone to scan the QR code shown on your computer.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D

10. When prompted to verify, approve the sign-in by entering the number shown (example shown: **62**) in Microsoft Authenticator.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D

## Email Code Verification (as shown in the source)

11. On the password screen, select **Email code**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D

12. On **We need to verify your identity**, select **Email** and click **Get code**.

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D

## Managing MFA Methods (as shown in the source)

13. In **Add a method**, open **Choose a method** and select the method you want to add (options shown: **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, **Office phone**).

  
https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D
