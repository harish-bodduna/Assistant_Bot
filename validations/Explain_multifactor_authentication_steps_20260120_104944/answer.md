# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup Guide (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

**Step 1: Accept the Microsoft invitation**
- Open the invitation email.
- Select **Accept invitation** (the email notes: *“If you were not expecting this invitation, proceed with caution.”*).



![Accept invitation](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)

**Step 2: Sign in to Microsoft**
- On the Microsoft sign-in page, enter your password.
- Select **Sign in**.



![Microsoft sign-in password](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D)

**Step 3: Accept the permissions prompt**
- Review the **Permissions requested by** prompt.
- Select **Accept**.



![Permissions requested - Accept](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)

**Step 4: Start Microsoft Authenticator setup**
- When you see **Keep your account secure** for **Microsoft Authenticator** and “Set up your account in the app,” select **Next**.
- Use **Show QR code** when prompted (as shown in the setup screens).



![Keep your account secure - Next](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

**Step 5: Display the QR code on your computer**
- On the setup page, display the QR code so it can be scanned by the Authenticator app.



![QR code displayed](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)

**Step 6: In Microsoft Authenticator, add your work account**
- On your phone, open **Microsoft Authenticator**.
- Tap **Add account** and choose **Work or school account**.



![Authenticator - Work or school account](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

**Step 7: Scan the QR code**
- Choose **Scan QR code** in the Authenticator app.
- Use your phone camera to scan the QR code shown on your computer.



![Authenticator - Scan QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

**Step 8: Approve the sign-in using number matching**
- When prompted, approve the notification in Microsoft Authenticator by entering the number shown on the **Keep your account secure** screen.



![Number matching example](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)
