# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Setup Guide (1440 Foods)

## Instructions

**Step 1 — Accept the Microsoft invitation email**  
Open the Microsoft invitation email and select **Accept invitation** (only proceed if you were expecting the invitation).

  
![Accept invitation email](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)

**Step 2 — Sign in with your password**  
On the Microsoft sign-in screen, enter your password and select **Sign in**.

  
![Enter password - Sign in](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_d6eb.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fU9pGBKo7Ox9UR404pyB54%2BtirNXNuUrugoblO/I8O0%3D)

**Step 3 — Accept the permissions request**  
Review the permissions requested and select **Accept** (only accept if you trust the organization shown).

  
![Permissions requested - Accept](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_564d.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=xgxVPf7zt5kDeG0WX6jBVsFJkhVL6nX4vqUSMKDj%2B2o%3D)

**Step 4 — Start Microsoft Authenticator setup (on your computer)**  
When you see **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator**, select **Next** to continue setup.

  
![Keep your account secure - Next](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D)

**Step 5 — Display the QR code (on your computer)**  
On the setup screen, select **Show QR code** to display the QR code you will scan with your phone.

  
![QR code displayed](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)

**Step 6 — Add your work/school account in Microsoft Authenticator (on your phone)**  
In Microsoft Authenticator, go to **Add account** and choose **Work or school account**.

  
![Authenticator - Work or school account](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_3e6e.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=f3zZ/1lY1mDoDITxInijBDF9Kif/%2BP/I3PPVRGN/TTY%3D)

**Step 7 — Scan the QR code (on your phone)**  
Choose **Scan QR code** and scan the QR code shown on your computer.

  
![Authenticator - Scan QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D)

**Step 8 — Approve the test notification (number matching)**  
When prompted, approve the notification by entering the number shown on-screen (example shown: **62**).

  
![Number matching prompt (example: 62)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_b5be.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=6EHQj3yGEXuAKQ026pEbXbU711dmJpMxG9ZY2HiOeRs%3D)

**Step 9 — (If prompted) Verify using an email code**  
If Microsoft prompts you to verify your identity by email, select **Get code**, then use the **Email code** option when signing in.

  
![Email code option on sign-in](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

  
![Verify identity - Get code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

**Step 10 — Add an additional verification method (optional)**  
If you are on the **Add a method** screen, use the dropdown to choose a method (examples shown include **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, **Office phone**).

  
![Add a method dropdown](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What if I wasn’t expecting the Microsoft invitation email?**  
  The invitation screen notes that you should **proceed with caution** if you were not expecting the invitation.
- **Should I accept the permissions request?**  
  The permissions screen states you should **only accept if you trust** the organization shown.
- **Need help?**  
  Contact IT Support: **ITsupport@1440foods.com** or **(646) 809-0885**.
