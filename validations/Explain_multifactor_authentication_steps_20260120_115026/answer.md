# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Steps (Microsoft Authenticator)

## Instructions

**Step 1: Accept the Microsoft invitation**
- In the invitation email, select **Accept invitation**.

  
![Invitation email – “Accept invitation”](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)

**Step 2: Sign in with your Microsoft password**
- On the Microsoft sign-in page, enter your password and select **Sign in**.

  
![Sign-in flow (see page)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=sqNzJVPL1NodpWdJJyz7XNhi%2BcE9B2JsS%2B3KzvdJGrY%3D)

**Step 3: Accept the requested permissions**
- On the **Permissions requested** screen, select **Accept**.

  
![Permissions requested (see page)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=sqNzJVPL1NodpWdJJyz7XNhi%2BcE9B2JsS%2B3KzvdJGrY%3D)

**Step 4: Begin Microsoft Authenticator setup**
- On **Keep your account secure — Microsoft Authenticator**, select **Next**.
- If needed, select **Show QR code**.

  
![“Keep your account secure” – Next / Show QR code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_ab05.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=iyBsNYu5Hd6UA03mlhDLpLsGDMin%2B7uMDE05JXHgrR0%3D)

**Step 5: Display the QR code on your computer**
- Ensure the QR code is visible on the **Set up your account in the app** screen.

  
![QR code displayed](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)

**Step 6: In Microsoft Authenticator, add your work/school account**
- In the Authenticator app, choose **Add account** and select **Work or school account**.

  
![Authenticator – “Work or school account”](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_dfc8.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=WCCXUgKCAlacEy5cqzBhP8nW1QUozHQLFbDqgKeLSt0%3D)

**Step 7: Choose “Scan QR code”**
- Select **Scan QR code** in the Authenticator app.

  
![Authenticator – “Scan QR code”](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0ad0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A56Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A56Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=G4BIwEsY93EN0PsYmCC2DnFJZMgI5iJ%2BzdnY2uemlAM%3D)

**Step 8: Scan the QR code**
- Use your phone to scan the QR code displayed on your computer.

  
![QR code displayed](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_c0cf.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=nb7exvchy0tbC5pBIJFQXRM4be2AY2KBnHXM3M%2Bd36w%3D)

**Step 9: Approve the test notification (number matching)**
- Approve the notification by entering the number shown on the setup screen.

  
![Number-matching approval example](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_5d9c.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=EyXu4VvGWNE6LicrKFQrk0F1E5ewU522MCxh0c1l%2BlI%3D)

## Optional: Email verification (if prompted)

**Step 10: Request an email security code**
- On **We need to verify your identity**, choose **Email** and select **Get code**.

  
![Verify identity – Get code](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_0657.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=bg2P9/5x9gPj%2BhTAePgeVETeuSNuTys0D1ROluHBpnE%3D)

**Step 11: Enter the email code and continue**
- Enter the code and continue sign-in.

  
![Enter password / email code option](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

## Optional: Add another MFA method

**Step 12: Add a method**
- In **Add a method**, open **Choose a method** and select one of the available options shown (for example: **Authenticator app**, **Email**, **Security key**, **Office phone**).

  
![Add a method – Choose a method](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_1c28.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=GuZwBVaFYmSl4zPEs5r08Sgxfj7c6UXjc0/W9rqkYXY%3D)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What MFA methods are shown as available to add?**  
  - Authenticator app  
  - Email  
  - Security key  
  - Office phone
- **Where do I get the QR code for the Authenticator app?**  
  - On the **Set up your account in the app** screen; use **Show QR code** if it’s hidden.
