# Microsoft Multifactor Authentication (MFA) Steps

## Instructions (Microsoft Authenticator / QR Code)

1. Open the Microsoft invitation email and click **Accept invitation**.

   
![Accept invitation (page 4)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_61b0.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A58Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A58Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=goiVgAly5GRpRluvXs8kbWnxhI/RVFG9%2BMRNrEGSt4w%3D)

2. On the Microsoft sign-in screen, enter your password and click **Sign in**.

   
![Sign in (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)

3. If you see a **Permissions requested** prompt, review it and click **Accept**.

   
![Permissions requested (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)

4. When prompted with **Keep your account secure** for **Microsoft Authenticator**, click **Next**.

   
![Keep your account secure - Next (page 3)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

5. Select **Show QR code** to display the QR code on your screen.

   
![Show QR code (page 3)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

6. On your phone, on the **Add account** screen, select **Work or school account**.

   
![Add account - Work or school account (page 2)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_9bd9.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=fHGoPuQ4wzN3Zx/xPGvE/mcYHkm596aOwguVCD/yl3M%3D)

7. Choose **Scan QR code** on your phone.

   
![Scan QR code (page 2)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/assets/Image_1c28.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A57Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A57Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=GuZwBVaFYmSl4zPEs5r08Sgxfj7c6UXjc0/W9rqkYXY%3D)

8. Use your phone to scan the QR code shown on your computer to pair your account.

   
![QR code on screen (page 3)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

9. When prompted to approve a notification, enter the number shown on the screen in Microsoft Authenticator (example shown: **62**) to approve.

   
![Number prompt example (page 3)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A55Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A55Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=O73HKiMp08Bqha0V3rKqskdWe8rDrFTfJllG%2B6zb63o%3D)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I’m asked to verify by email instead?
If you see prompts like **Email code** or **We need to verify your identity** with a **Get code** button, follow the on-screen steps to receive a code by email and enter it to continue.

   
![Email verification prompts (page 1)](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/1440-Microsoft_Multifactor_Authentication_Documentation/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A33%3A54Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A38%3A54Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=eZBhBZ3EHegldstuvdLjo/hB9Pyq8FCs840bQFFzb/I%3D)
