# External SharePoint Access (1440 Marketing External)

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the SharePoint invitation email
1. Open the email from **SharePoint Online** (`no-reply@sharepointonline.com`) indicating access to **1440 Marketing External**.
2. Click **Go To 1440 Marketing External**.

  
![Page 1](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_1.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=SZSPk9RPXhMfhHoSlEs0ZSZwNu4%2BBKdxvrw9NXthWmg%3D)

---

### Step 2 — Continue when prompted for additional security information
1. If you see **More information required**, select **Next** to continue.

  
![Page 2](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_2.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=grkfawu6B/xtFLrad4Uczt7VDHNeSCGV704cyL5HI7c%3D)

---

### Step 3 — Sign in using the emailed code
1. On the Microsoft **Sign in** screen, select **Send code**.
2. When you receive the email code, enter it on the **Enter code** screen.
3. Select **Sign in**.

  
![Page 2](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_2.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=grkfawu6B/xtFLrad4Uczt7VDHNeSCGV704cyL5HI7c%3D)

---

### Step 4 — Accept the permissions request
1. On **Permissions requested by: 1440 Foods (1440foods.onmicrosoft.com)**, review the requested permissions.
2. Select **Accept**.

  
![Page 2](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_2.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=grkfawu6B/xtFLrad4Uczt7VDHNeSCGV704cyL5HI7c%3D)

---

### Step 5 — Complete Microsoft Authenticator setup (if shown)
1. On **Keep your account secure** (Microsoft Authenticator), follow the prompts and select **Next** as you proceed.
2. When shown **Scan the QR code**, scan the QR code with Microsoft Authenticator, then select **Next**.
3. Continue through **Set up your account** and select **Next**.
4. On **Let’s try it out**, approve the notification by entering the number shown.
5. When you see **Notification approved**, select **Next**.
6. When you see **Success!**, select **Done**.

  
![Page 3](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_3.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=1GFFQv43K2nd0jR1PU2IorA4d3A%2BqwBBXdlaNU4TH9M%3D)

  
![Page 4](https://copilot14.blob.core.windows.net/documents-and-assets/qdrant-ingest-docs/External_SharePoint_access/pages/page_4.png?st=2026-01-19T20%3A36%3A09Z&se=2027-01-19T20%3A41%3A09Z&sp=r&sv=2026-02-06&sr=b&sig=aWg8RaL2TNRc31nFSyOqEAmdUpcfEn6VH/zGfHwitGM%3D)

---

### Step 6 — Confirm you’ve reached the SharePoint site
1. After completing sign-in and any security prompts, you should land on the SharePoint site **1440 Marketing External** (Document Center).
